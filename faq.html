Frequently Asked Questions

Detailed answers regarding hand-linked seamless construction, fiber origin, wash protocols, sizing tolerances, and our private Manhattan salon.

Standard industrial socks are closed at the toe using high-speed overlock sewing machines that fold the fabric together and stitch through it, creating a thick, raised ridge of thread that sits directly over your toenails. True seamless hand linking (*rimaglio*) involves taking the open loops of knit stitches from the top and bottom of the toe and mounting them onto rotating needles to stitch them together loop-by-loop with a single thread. This creates an entirely flat, continuous knitted fabric with zero ridge to cause friction or chafing inside your footwear.
The needle count refers to the number of microscopic needles arranged in the circular knitting cylinder. Standard mass-produced athletic socks typically use 96 to 144 needles, resulting in thick, coarse stitches. Our luxury dress hosiery is knitted on 200 to 240 needle cylinders using ultra-fine two-ply yarns. This ultra-dense knit creates a silky, lustrous fabric surface that fits smoothly against the foot like a second skin, resists pilling, and retains its elasticity over hundreds of wash cycles.
For pure cashmere and fine merino hosiery, we recommend hand washing in cool water using a gentle, pH-neutral wool wash, or machine washing on a delicate wool cycle inside a breathable mesh wash bag. Never use hot water, bleach, or fabric softeners, which coat natural fibers and impair their breathability. Always lay socks flat on a clean dry towel to air dry away from direct heat or tumble dryers, as mechanical heat agitation causes natural animal fibers to shrink and felt.
Fil d'Écosse (literally 'Scottish thread') refers to long-staple Egyptian mako cotton that has undergone double mercerization. The raw cotton yarn is pulled taut and bathed in a caustic sodium hydroxide solution, which rounds out the kidney-shaped cotton fibers, increases their tensile strength by 25%, and endows the yarn with an iridescent silk-like luster. Socks crafted from Fil d'Écosse are exceptionally durable, colorfast, and breathable, making them the gold standard of formal men's hosiery.
Our socks employ three engineered design elements to guarantee stay-up performance: first, an anatomical Y-gore heel pocket that anchors the sock securely under the calcaneus; second, graduated compression knitting that tapers gently up the calf; and third, a double-cuff welt knit with soft, micro-encapsulated core-spun elastane that hugs the leg without restricting venous blood circulation or leaving tight red indentation rings.
