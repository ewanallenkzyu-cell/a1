# Design System Registry • Seamless Hosiery

## Visual Concept: Sartorial Circular Knitting & Haute Bonneterie
An Italian luxury textile aesthetic inspired by Brescia hosiery workshops, loop-by-loop hand linking, and two-ply natural fiber curation.

### Color Palette Tokens
- **Terracotta Accent**: `#c25e40` (Primary accents, borders, and craft badges)
- **Terracotta Hover**: `#a84e34` (Interactive hover states)
- **Cashmere Ecru**: `#f8f6f0` (Primary warm background canvas)
- **Oat Silk Surface**: `#ffffff` (Card container surfaces)
- **Parchment Alt**: `#f1ede4` (Secondary tinted background sections)
- **Merino Charcoal**: `#1a1e24` (Primary typography and bold headers)
- **Dark Base**: `#15191f` (Footer foundation and high-contrast night accents)
- **Border Natural**: `#dfd8cb` (Tailoring hairline dividers)
- **Muted Textile**: `#5a6472` (Subtitles, body copy, and fiber specifications)

### Typography Hierarchy
- **Display & Headings**: `Italiana` (Google Fonts via `<link>`), font weight 400.
- **Body & Technical UI**: `Plus Jakarta Sans` (Google Fonts via `<link>`), font weights 300, 400, 500, 600, 700.

### Structural Framework
- Max Container Width: `1240px`
- Responsive Breakpoints: `1024px` (Tablet / Laptop), `768px` (Mobile Drawer)
- Card Shadow Tokens: `0 4px 15px rgba(26,30,36,0.04)` to `0 20px 45px rgba(26,30,36,0.12)`
