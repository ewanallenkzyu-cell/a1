4. Product Suitability & Footwear Compatibility Protocols

Our luxury seamless hosiery and fine cashmere socks are designed as premium sartorial apparel intended for refined lifestyle wear, business tailoring, and celebratory evening occasions. These delicate knits are not engineered for coarse industrial boots, barefoot outdoor walking over abrasive concrete, or aggressive high-friction athletic sports requiring specialized synthetics. Wearing ultra-fine 240-needle cashmere socks inside rough, unlined work boots can cause premature heel friction and surface fiber shearing.

Seamless Hosiery disclaims liability for personal property damages resulting from improper footwear pairing, contact with corrosive chemicals, sharp friction surfaces, or severe laundry shrinkage caused by commercial high-heat laundering. Patrons should consult our care guidelines to ensure their creations receive appropriate preventative maintenance and gentle handling over years of daily wear. By acquiring our products, clients acknowledge the specialized care requirements inherent to artisanal luxury knitwear goods.
